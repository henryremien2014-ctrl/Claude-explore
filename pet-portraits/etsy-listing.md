create the listing; you upload the finished files to the order when it's done.

## Description (Royal version; swap the style paragraph for the others)

```
Turn your best friend into royalty. 👑

I'll transform your favorite photo of your dog, cat (or any pet!) into a
regal Renaissance-style portrait, made just for you and delivered digitally,
ready to print, frame or share.

WHAT YOU GET
• A high-resolution portrait file (4:5, 4K), which prints well up to 16×20 in
• A phone wallpaper version (9:16)
• Upgrade to a LIVING PORTRAIT and your portrait gently comes to life in a
  5-second animated video (see the Living Portrait listing)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Place your order and choose your options
2. Send me 1–3 clear photos of your pet through Etsy Messages
3. I create your portrait and check it carefully against your photos
4. Your files arrive in your Etsy account within 1–3 business days
   (or 24 hours with Rush)

PHOTO TIPS
Bright natural light, eye-level, face clearly visible, and no heavy
filters. Your pet's markings are the most important thing to get right, so
send photos that show them clearly.

ONE FREE REVISION, AND A LIKENESS PROMISE
If something doesn't look right, tell me and I'll fix it. If I can't capture
your pet, you'll get a full refund.

HOW IT'S MADE
I create each portrait from your photo using AI image tools, then
personally review every one for likeness and quality before it's sent. This
is a digital product; no physical item will be shipped.

PRINTING
Print at home, at any photo lab, or at an online print shop. Fits standard
8×10, 11×14 and 16×20 frames.
```

## FAQ (Etsy shop FAQ section)

- **Can you do other animals?** Yes: rabbits, horses, birds, reptiles and more.
- **My pet has passed away. Can you still do it?** Yes. Send your favorite photos and I'll treat them with care.
- **Do I get a physical print?** No. This is a digital file you can print anywhere.
- **Can I use it for a Christmas card?** Yes, it's yours for personal use.
- **Is AI used?** Yes. I use AI image tools guided by your photo, and I check and select every result myself.
