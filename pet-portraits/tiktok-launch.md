# TikTok launch plan

Goal: get people to your Etsy shop. Post 1 video a day, 7–15 seconds each,
with the hook in the first second.

## Setup

- Switch to a Business account so you can put the Etsy link in your bio.
- Turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label for every portrait video.
- Only feature customers' pets with their permission (message template C).

## First 10 videos

| # | Video | Hook (on-screen text) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pet photo → Royal portrait → it blinks (Living Portrait) | "I turned my dog into an 18th-century duke 👑" |
| 2 | The same pet in all 3 styles, side by side | "Which one should I print? 1, 2 or 3?" |
| 3 | Slow zoom on the Living Portrait | "Wait for it… 👀" |
| 4 | Real photo, then the portrait, showing that the markings match | "Every spot in exactly the right place" |
| 5 | A draft where the AI got it wrong, next to the fixed version | "AI tried to give my cat an extra ear 💀" |
| 6 | Customer reveal (with permission) | "Bella's mom's reaction to her royal portrait" |
| 7 | "Your pet in the comments": make one free each week from comments | "Comment your pet, I'll paint one this week" |
| 8 | A watercolor portrait, calm music (memorial angle; only with permission) | "For everyone missing a best friend 🌈" |
| 9 | Christmas edition, from Nov 1 | "POV: your dog's Christmas card this year" |
| 10 | The printed portrait on a real wall | "From a phone photo to this" |

## Rules

- Put the reveal in the first 1–2 seconds; don't make viewers wait.
- Use trending sounds, but only from TikTok's library.
- Reply to comments with video replies (#7 builds on this).
- Check weekly: which style gets the most views? Make more of that, and list it first on Etsy.
- Checkpoint Oct 29: if the average video is under ~500 views, change the hook format before changing anything else.
