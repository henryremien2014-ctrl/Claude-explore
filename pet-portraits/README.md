# Pet Portrait Shop — launch kit

Custom digital pet portraits sold on Etsy, made from the buyer's photo with AI
image tools (Higgsfield), checked by hand for likeness, and delivered as
high-resolution files. The premium tier adds a **Living Portrait**: the
finished portrait gently animated as a short looping video.

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [`styles.md`](styles.md) | The 3 signature styles, generation prompts, animation prompts, quality checklist |
| [`etsy-listing.md`](etsy-listing.md) | Titles, tags, description, FAQ, personalization text, AI disclosure |
| [`order-workflow.md`](order-workflow.md) | Step-by-step order handling and message templates for buyers |
| [`tiktok-launch.md`](tiktok-launch.md) | 10 launch videos and posting rules |

## Offer and prices (starting point — adjust after the first 20 sales)

| Product | Price | What the buyer gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Pet Portrait** (1 pet) | $24 | Print-ready 4:5 file (4K; prints well up to 16×20 in) + phone-wallpaper version |
| **Living Portrait** (1 pet) | $36 | Everything above + a 5-second animated video of the portrait (MP4) |
| Extra pet in the same portrait | +$10 | |
| 24-hour rush | +$8 | |

Every order includes one free revision. If the portrait can't be made to look
like the pet, the buyer gets a full refund.

## Costs

- Etsy: $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or on each sale), about 6.5%
  transaction fee, plus payment processing of roughly 3% + $0.25. Etsy's
  offsite ads take 15% of a sale they bring in.
- Higgsfield: already on the Plus plan. Credits per order will be measured on
  the first samples and written here.
- Start-up spend: about $2 in listing fees. No ads until the shop sells on its own.

## Setup checklist

**You (only you can do these):**
- [ ] Open an Etsy shop and set up Etsy Payments. Etsy sellers must be 18+, or
      have a parent or guardian run the account.
- [ ] Pick a shop name and check it's free on Etsy and TikTok. Ideas: *Noble
      Paws Studio*, *Whisker & Crown*, *Painted Paw Co*, *Pocket Pet Portraits*.
      Avoid names that include brands or characters.
- [ ] Upload 5–10 clear photos of a real pet to Higgsfield (see
      `order-workflow.md` → "Getting photos to Claude"). Use your own pet, or a

## Rules that keep the shop safe

- Disclose AI use in every listing (Etsy requires it). The wording is in `etsy-listing.md`.
- No brand names, characters, or "in the style of [studio]" in titles, tags or
  prompts. That includes Disney, Pixar and similar studios.
- Never post fake reviews or offer anything in exchange for a review.
- Only post a customer's pet on TikTok with their permission.
